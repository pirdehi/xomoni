# `modules/dashboard`: devices and live job status

Shows the signed-in person which browsers have the extension connected and how their jobs are doing, updating live: jobs that need the person come first (_Needs you_), then each X account's risk level (#41). It never starts or approves work on X. The changes it can make are a one-time code that connects a browser, typed or handed to the extension that asked on `/connect` ([ADR-0024](../../../../../docs/adr/0024-connect-a-browser-with-one-click.md)), ([ADR-0018](../../../../../docs/adr/0018-pair-the-extension-with-a-one-time-code.md)), disconnecting a device ([ADR-0006](../../../../../docs/adr/0006-website-is-view-only.md)), and asking Xomoni in the same browser to pause, cancel or resume one of its jobs, or to open one of its pages; a resume is approved only in Xomoni ([ADR-0027](../../../../../docs/adr/0027-extension-actions-from-the-website.md), #251).

## Data flow

1. `app/dashboard/page.tsx` (Overview) and `app/dashboard/[section]/page.tsx` (`sectionOr404`) render `app/dashboard/screen.tsx`, which calls `requireViewer()` (auth module) and `loadDashboardSnapshot()`.
2. The page renders `<Dashboard initial={snapshot}>`.
3. `useLiveDashboard` subscribes to Realtime changes filtered by the user's id, on a private channel `dashboard:<user id>` that a policy on `realtime.messages` opens to its owner only (#81). Realtime and PostgREST both enforce Row Level Security.
4. A browser connects with a code from `PairingPanel`: the extension trades it for a device token (`pair_device`) and reports through `device_report`; the new device and its jobs arrive over the same live feed.
5. _Needs you_ and _X accounts_ are derived from the jobs on every change, so they update live too. An account appears only once a job reported its level; the level is per browser, because each browser's extension keeps its own risk ladder.

## Same numbers as the extension (#239)

A job card counts as the extension's does: _Removed_, _Skipped_ and _Failed_ (each only when there are some), and _Remaining_ (`jobRemaining`), with the percentage next to the bar. The account bar names the person and says whether updates are live; the account page and _Sign out_ are in the header's menu.

## One sign-in (#232, ADR-0025)

- `/connect`, signed in, signs the extension in at once: no _Connect this browser_ click. A failure says why and offers _Try again_. It keeps the extension's id for the site (`rememberExtensionId`, from `modules/extension`).
- The browser list marks _This browser_ when its Xomoni reports as one of the account's devices (`useExtensionLink`), and offers no _Disconnect_ for it: signing out disconnects it.
- _Finish connecting_ hides once Xomoni here is signed in.
