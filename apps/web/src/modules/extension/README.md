# `modules/extension`: the Xomoni extension in this browser

Finds the Xomoni extension installed in the same browser, keeps xomoni.com and the extension signed in and out together: one sign-in (#232, [ADR-0025](../../../../../docs/adr/0025-one-sign-in-for-the-website-and-the-extension.md)), and hands it the website's actions, or says in one dialog why it can't (#251, [ADR-0027](../../../../../docs/adr/0027-extension-actions-from-the-website.md)). It talks to the extension through Chrome's `externally_connectable`, with the messages in [`@xomoni/bridge`](../../../../../packages/bridge/README.md). Nothing here sends X content to a server ([ADR-0004](../../../../../docs/adr/0004-x-data-stays-local.md)): what a page reads from Xomoni (the Library, Watch, account health) stays in that page's memory, never in storage, logs or reports ([ADR-0028](../../../../../docs/adr/0028-xomoni-data-on-the-website-in-the-same-browser.md), #233). Nothing can start or approve work on X: an action pauses or cancels a job, opens a page of Xomoni, or asks the person to resume in Xomoni ([ADR-0027](../../../../../docs/adr/0027-extension-actions-from-the-website.md)).

## Layers

| Layer | Files | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `domain/` | `session-sync.ts`, `link.ts`, `presence.ts` | `xomoniPresence`: whether Xomoni can act from this page (here, checking, not installed, a phone, signed out, another account), here only for one of the account's own devices; `isPhoneBrowser`; `jobActionsFor` (pause and cancel while a job runs, resume and cancel while it waits, none once it ended); `answerMessage`, Xomoni's answer in plain words. `decideSync`: what brings the two into step, the newest action winning (signed in here → sign the extension in or switch it; left in the extension after this session started → sign the website out; signed out here after the extension connected → sign it out). `extensionIdIn` and `withoutExtensionId`: the `#ext=<id>` fragment the extension puts on pages it opens. |
| `application/` | `ports.ts`, `session-sync.ts`, `actions.ts` | Ports `ExtensionChannel`, `ActionChannel`, `BrowserMemory`, `AccountSession`, `SyncLock`. `askXomoni`: hands one request to the extension the site knows, or answers `no_answer` without sending. `readXomoni`: the same for a read (ADR-0028), null without an extension. `syncSession`: asks the extension how it stands, decides and acts; keeps the session's first sighting on every page. `signOutBoth`: remembers a website sign-out and signs the extension out too. |
| `infrastructure/` | `chrome-extension-channel.ts`, `browser-memory.ts`, `supabase-account-session.ts`, `web-lock.ts` | `chrome.runtime.sendMessage` with timeouts and the strict parsers, for the sign-in, the actions (`xomoni-job`, `xomoni-open`) and the reads (`xomoni-read`, 15 seconds); the site's `localStorage` (`xomoni.extension`, `xomoni.signedOutAt`, `xomoni.session`), safe when storage is blocked; the session id from the access token (decoded, not verified), the account's device ids, one-time codes and this session's sign-out on the browser Supabase client; one sync at a time across tabs with Web Locks. |
| `client/` | `extension-session.ts`, `xomoni-actions.ts` | Browser composition roots: the sync and the link state pages read (`useExtensionLink`): unknown, none, unreachable, signed in (device and masked account) or signed out; and `createXomoniActionServices` (ask, read, and look again with a sync). |
| `ui/` | `extension-session-sync.tsx` | `ExtensionSessionSync`, in the root layout on every page: keeps the `#ext=` id and clears it from the address bar, syncs when a page opens, comes back into view or the browser signs in or out (not on `/connect`, which pairs by itself), goes to `/login` when a sign-out in the extension signed the website out, and signs the extension out before any form posts to `/auth/signout`. Re-exports `rememberExtensionId` and `useExtensionLink` for other modules. `xomoni-actions.tsx`: `useXomoni` (a page's requests: sent to Xomoni here, with a notice of its answer, or the one dialog; a click while the page is still looking for Xomoni waits for it, up to `CHECK_WAIT_MS`), `XomoniDialog` (not installed: install steps, *Install Xomoni* and *I've installed it*; a phone: *Send to my computer*, through the share sheet or the clipboard; signed out or another account: *Connect this browser* and *I've signed in*; a job of another browser; it follows the live state), `XomoniJobActions` and `OpenInXomoni`. `useXomoni(...).read` asks only while Xomoni is here, naming its device; `useXomoniRead` reads a place again when its request changes or on `reload`, drops an answer that comes after the page moved on, and forgets it once Xomoni is gone. |

## Used by

- `app/layout.tsx`: `ExtensionSessionSync` on every page.
- `modules/dashboard/ui`: `/connect` keeps the extension's id (`rememberExtensionId`); the dashboard marks *This browser* and hides the finished *Finish connecting* notice (`useExtensionLink`); job cards and places that need Xomoni use `useXomoni`, `XomoniJobActions` and `OpenInXomoni` (#251).

## Rules

- The usual layer rules (`npm run arch`). `domain/` and `application/` may import `@xomoni/bridge`, a pure contract (ADR-0025).
- Times compared by `decideSync` all come from this computer's clock: the extension's and the site's own records, never the server's.
