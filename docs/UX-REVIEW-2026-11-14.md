# Dashboard UX review

> The whole dashboard, page by page, as a person who wants a cleaner X account meets it, and what Phase 12 changes. Reviewed on 14 November 2026, on `main` after [#197](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/pull/197), with the demo data of `npm run preview:ui` and the maintainer's real dashboard.

## The person and the job

Someone who wants old posts, replies, reposts and likes gone, without risking the account, and a copy of what they had. They open Xomoni now and then: to start a cleanup, to see how it is going, and to step in when X asks for them. They are not interested in X's rate limits, sweeps or inventories. Xomoni should tell them three things at a glance:

- whether all is well;
- what is happening now;
- what to do next.

## What gets in the way

### The dashboard has more places than tasks

- **Seven sections** for three tasks (clean up, keep a copy, step in when X asks):
  - *Overview*, *Cleanup*, *Library*, *Watch*, *Jobs*, *Activity log* and *Settings*.
  - *Jobs* and *Activity log* both answer "what happened?".
  - *Watch* is a separate tool that reads X's search.
- **The same action starts in many places.** *New cleanup* is on Overview, Jobs and Cleanup. *Get my archive* is on the Library and in a notice on Cleanup. *Complete my copy* and *Read one part* are on the Library.

### Overview shows numbers, not the state of things

- **Overview has six blocks of equal weight:**
  - five counters;
  - a chart by year;
  - *Limits & pacing*;
  - the job in progress;
  - recent activity.
- **Nothing says whether all is well or what to do next.** A first-time visitor sees zeros and a chart.
- **Counts that disagree side by side.** *Removed by Xomoni 337*, *Replies 1,500 · 337 removed*, and *X shows 8,421 posts on your profile*. The 1,225 posts Xomoni knows don't match the 8,421 X shows, and nothing says why.
- ***Limits & pacing* is for experts and reads as alarming.**
  - It shows per-hour and per-day bars and 15-minute windows.
  - *Search 46 used · stops at 10 of 50* in red reads like an error. It means Xomoni stops at a fifth of X's 50.

### Cleanup asks for decisions only an expert can make

- **The four steps are clear until step 4, *How to find them*.** There the person chooses between *Sweep X search* and *Deep clean from inventory*, which are Xomoni's own mechanics, and both are on by default anyway.
- ***Keep* is good, but always open.** Three fields show even when the person keeps nothing.

### The Library says the same thing twice

- **The archive appears twice:**
  - in *Your copy*, as one of three sources;
  - in *X's archive*, as a card of four steps.
- **Two primary buttons sit side by side:** *Complete my copy* and *Get my archive*.

### Settings opens on the expert's view

- ***Pacing* is a table of 35 number fields** (five actions × seven values) at the top of Settings, though nearly everyone stays on *Careful*.
- ***Pause after this many failures in a row* sits among everyday settings.**

### Words from inside the engine

- **Job cards show Xomoni's own terms.** Examples: *phase: sweep*, and *Account health: Risk level 1 (Caution): X rate-limited Xomoni in the last hour. During this run X sent one rate limit. Sample checks: 4 confirmed and 1 still to run.*
- **Several names for the same things:**
  - *X worker*, *X window* and *Xomoni window* are one window;
  - *inventory*, *copy* and *Library* are one copy.
- **Most cards open with a paragraph of grey text.** Together they make every page read like a manual.

### What needs the person is easy to miss

- **The warning that the X window must stay visible is a single truncated line** at the bottom of the sidebar (*Keep the X worker vis…*), on the maintainer's real dashboard.
- **When X asks for a password or a check, the signal is in the job card,** on whichever page holds it.

### The Activity log is long and flat

- **One line per action** (*Deleted*, *Deleted*, *Deleted*…), without the post or a grouping, so a day of work is hundreds of identical lines.

### The look

- **Fluent UI is used correctly but plainly:**
  - every card has the same weight and border;
  - most text is small and grey;
  - the main numbers and actions don't stand out from the explanations.

## What stays

- **The safety rules** and the one-fifth caps ([ADR-0012](adr/0012-one-fifth-of-every-x-limit.md)).
- **Informed consent.**
- **The Cleanup preview,** with how many posts match and how long it will take.
- **The archive's four steps.**
- **Light and dark themes,** and **Fluent UI** ([ADR-0019](adr/0019-fluent-ui-everywhere.md)).
- **Every number and setting stays reachable.** They move behind *Advanced* or *Details*, nothing is removed.

## Phase 12 · A simpler dashboard

Each task is an issue and a pull request with tests, in this order:

| # | Task | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Settings, presets first** ([#198](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/198)) | *Careful* explained in one line; the pacing table only under *Custom*; *Pause after failures* under *Advanced*. |
| 2 | **Cleanup in three questions** ([#199](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/199)) | What, how old, and start. *Keep* folds into *Exceptions (optional)* with a one-line summary. *How to find them* moves under *Advanced*, on by default. |
| 3 | **Attention you can't miss** ([#200](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/200)) | A banner at the top of every page when X needs the person or the X window must stay visible, with the one action that fixes it. |
| 4 | **Home answers "what now?"** ([#201](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/201)) | Overview becomes *Home*:<br>• one status block (all well / what runs / the one next action);<br>• a first-run checklist (X window, archive, first cleanup);<br>• the counters in one compact row;<br>• the chart below.<br>*Limits & pacing* moves to Settings. |
| 5 | **Jobs and Activity log in one place** ([#202](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/202)) | One *Activity* page with *Jobs* and *Log*; the menu goes from seven sections to six. |
| 6 | **One card for your copy** ([#203](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/203)) | The Library shows how complete the copy is in one card, the archive as the recommended way, and the reads as *Other ways*. |
| 7 | **An Activity log you can read** ([#204](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/204)) | Repeated lines grouped by job and day (*Deleted 31 posts, 01:01–01:32*). The post's text shows when Xomoni has it. |
| 8 | **Plain words** ([#205](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/205)) | Job cards without engine terms; one name for each thing (*X window*, *your copy*); helper text cut to one line, with details on demand. |
| 9 | **One set of numbers** ([#206](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/206)) | Each count says what it counts (*on X*, *in your copy*, *removed by Xomoni*), with no two disagreeing on one screen. |
| 10 | **A modern look** ([#207](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/207)) | One visual pass over page headers, card weight and spacing, type scale, status colours, empty states and the sidebar, in light and dark. |

**Done when:**

- Someone new can start a cleanup and get their archive without reading a paragraph.
- Every page has one primary action.
- `npm run verify` passes, with tests for each change.
