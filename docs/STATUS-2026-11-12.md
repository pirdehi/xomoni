### Limits that stay (decided, not open tasks)

- **The Xomoni dashboard must be open** for every job, the archive's looks included: the engine runs in it.
- **X takes a day or more to make the archive**, and may ask for the password again before the download.
- **X's language:** the archive buttons are pressed only by their English labels. In other languages the person presses the button the job names.
- **The extension's interface is English.** A Persian, right-to-left interface was offered as later work.
- **The risk isn't zero:** X's rules forbid non-API automation. Xomoni lowers the risk with the one-fifth caps, the warm-up and a stop at the first warning, but can't remove it.

### Dropped

- **Assisted mode, official API mode and the mode chooser** ([#35](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/35), [#36](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/36), [#37](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/37)): on 1 November it was decided to keep automatic mode only, at one fifth of X's limits.
- **Hiding automation from X** ([#47](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/47)): never ([ADR-0003](adr/0003-no-credentials-no-evasion.md)).
