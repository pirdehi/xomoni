# `shared/vault/`: X's official files, kept on the computer

The vault ([ADR-0043](../../../../../docs/adr/0043-the-vault-x-files-kept-on-the-computer.md), #424): what X's official files say about the person's own account, read in, checked and kept in the extension's IndexedDB. It is part of `shared/`, the kernel, and follows its rules. Nothing here sends anything anywhere ([ADR-0004](../../../../../docs/adr/0004-x-data-stays-local.md)), and nothing here acts on X: it never sends a request to X, scrolls, navigates, clicks or opens a tab there.

## Files

| `types.ts`   | The data model and its closed lists: `VAULT_SOURCES`, the import ledger's row (`VaultImport`, with `ImportCounts`), X's analytics of a post (`AnalyticsPost`, `POST_METRICS`) and of a day (`AnalyticsDay`, `DAY_METRICS`), and the timeline's events (`AccountEvent`, `ACCOUNT_EVENT_KINDS`, `ACCOUNT_EVENT_SOURCES`), with their limits (`EVENT_WORDS`, 500; `FINGERPRINT`; `MAX_COUNT`, 10¹²). | `records.ts` | Every vault row checked and copied field by field (`vaultImportRecord`, `analyticsPostRecord`, `analyticsDayRecord`, `accountEventRecord`): X ids, whole numbers from 0 to 10¹², real days that aren't in the future (`isVaultDay`), known kinds and sources, texts of limited size, refs that can't carry markup. `cleanWords` cleans the timeline's words and cuts them to 500. Used by `db.ts` here and by the move file (ADR-0032). Pure.                              |
| `db.ts`      | Storage, on the tables of Dexie version 8 (`../db.ts`): the ledger (`recordImport`, `findImport` for duplicates, `importLedger`), the analytics merges (`mergeAnalyticsPosts`: each number the highest seen, never lower; `mergeAnalyticsDays`: the newer import wins), the timeline (`addAccountEvent`, once per kind and `ref` within `EVENT_WINDOWS`; `accountTimeline`, newest first, joined with the risk ladder's events in `RISK_EVENT_WORDS`). One bad row writes nothing (`VaultError`). |
| `importer.ts` | The one importer (ADR-0043, decisions 1 to 5): `openFiles` lists what was dropped (a ZIP's directory only, its entries read in place; an entry whose path climbs out, `isSafeZipPath`, is left out), `registerParser` takes a strategy per kind of file (`VaultParser`: `matches`, which may peek at a file's first bytes with `head` within its limit, `onePerFile` for a part per file, `maxBytes`, `owner`, which sees the files' fingerprint, `read` and `finish`), `planImport` gives each file to the first parser that takes it, checks every size before anything is read, asks whose files they are (refused, or to confirm), and looks each part's `fingerprint` up in the ledger (a ZIP's: the paths, sizes and CRCs of its directory; a text file up to 50 MB: its SHA-256 by Web Crypto; a folder: its files' paths, sizes and times). `runImport` merges batch by batch and writes one ledger row per part: a duplicate writes only that row, a cancel (`AbortSignal`) stops between batches and keeps what was merged, a failure writes `failed` and throws. |
| `archive-manifest.ts` | `parseThar`: X's archive manifest (`data/manifest.js`, `window.__THAR_CONFIG`) read tolerantly: when X made the archive, whether it is partial, its size and each kind of data's count; an unknown field is ignored, a broken file is null. Pure. |
| `archive-parser.ts` | X's archive on the importer (`ARCHIVE_PARSER`, registered when the module loads): `account.js`, `tweets.js`, `like.js` and their parts, and the manifest, with the archive import's own limits (`MAX_ARCHIVE_FILE_BYTES`, `MAX_ACCOUNT_FILE_BYTES`), words and chunks of 20,000 (`ARCHIVE_IMPORT_CHUNK`, #390). The ledger keeps the date X made the archive (`coverTo`), and the timeline an `archive_imported` entry. |
| `csv.ts` | `parseCsv` (#425, RFC 4180): a header and rows, each with its line; quoted cells with `""`, line breaks inside quotes, a byte order mark, CRLF, LF or CR, and a comma, semicolon or tab guessed from the header (`guessDelimiter`); a quote that never closes drops its record, named in `problems`; at most `MAX_CSV_ROWS` (500,000). Pure. `toCsvRow` writes one record for people (ADR-0044): RFC 4180 with CRLF, and a `'` before a cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return; `CSV_BOM` starts such a file. |
| `analytics-csv.ts` | X's analytics export, read (#425): `HEADER_ALIASES` (each field by every name X gave it, such as *Impressions* or *Views*, *Post id* or *Tweet id*), `mapHeaders` (and the columns it doesn't know), `classifyAnalyticsCsv` (posts, days or neither, with a confidence), `toNumber` (thousands separators, X's older `845.0`, a dash for unknown, anything negative bad), `toDay` (X's current, older, ISO and US dates), `analyticsPostRows`, `analyticsDayRows` and `parseReport`, which skips a row it can't read with its line and why, and gives null when nothing is X's analytics. Pure. |
| `analytics-parsers.ts` | X's analytics on the importer (#425): `ANALYTICS_DAYS_PARSER` and `ANALYTICS_POSTS_PARSER`, registered in that order, one part per CSV, 50 MB at most (`MAX_ANALYTICS_BYTES`), each CSV known by its header (`head`). A CSV of posts is the account's when at least half of its posts are in the inventory, refused below half, and confirmed by the person when the inventory has none; a CSV of days is always confirmed once, remembered for that file (`isConfirmed`, `rememberConfirmed` in `db.ts`). Merged 2,000 rows at a time (`ANALYTICS_CHUNK`); a post's text is kept, cut to 1,000, only when the inventory doesn't hold it. |
| `timeline.ts` | The account's timeline (#425): how each kind of entry looks (`ACCOUNT_EVENT_WORDS`: its name, a generic icon and a tone, which features extend with their kinds) and who said it (`SOURCE_WORDS`), the kinds the person can add (`PERSON_KINDS`), Xomoni's own writers (`notePremium`: a change of X Premium on the person's own profile, once and never at the first sighting; `noteArchiveRequested`: once per request), which never fail the work they record, and `timelineCsv`, the timeline as a CSV a spreadsheet can't run. |
| `freshness.ts` | How fresh each of X's files is, and the local reminder (#425, ADR-0043, decision 9): a registry of sources (`registerFreshness`, each dating its last copy with `lastAt(accountId, now)`; the core's: X's archive every 30 days, dated by the day X made it, and the analytics export every 60), `freshnessOf` (`never`, `fresh`, `due`, `overdue`), `dueNudges`, and `vaultReminder` / `remindIfDue`: at most once a day per account by the person's calendar (`vaultNudged:` in `kv`), only within active hours, never while signed out or with the Settings switch off, quiet for 7 days after *Not now* (`snoozeVault`, `vaultSnooze:`). It uses the notifications Xomoni has: no `alarms`, no new permission; it never opens X or starts anything. |
| `under-the-hood.ts`, `label-glossary.ts`, `reach.ts`, `reach-stats.ts`, `reach-compare.ts`, `suspects.ts` | Reach diagnosis (#428, #429, ADR-0045), described in `../README.md`: X's *Under the hood* file read, its labels' glossary, each post's reach from the best source (`VAULT_REACH_SOURCES` reads `analyticsPosts` and the Premium changes of `accountEvents`), the statistics, the comparisons and the suspected posts. |

| `under-the-hood-freshness.ts` | The monthly reminder for X's *Under the hood* report (#428, #429), a source of `freshness.ts` registered when the module loads: the newest report is dated by the day X made it (the 3rd of the month after its month, `underTheHoodOutAt`), and the next is due 31 days on; a month that is over with fewer than 10 of the account's posts and replies in Xomoni (`ownPostsIn`, through `[accountId+kind+createdAt]`) moves it on a month, at most 24, since X makes no report for it. `../../ui/vault-import.ts` (through the parser) and `../../background/sw.ts` load it. |
| `under-the-hood-parser.ts` | `UNDER_THE_HOOD_PARSER` (`x-under-the-hood`, registered when the module loads, with its reminder): a `.json` dropped on its own, never one inside a ZIP, one part per file, 5 MB at most. | `parsers.ts` | `CORE_PARSERS`: X's archive, then X's analytics of days, then of posts, registered in that order when imported; the Vault page offers a file to them first (#425). |

## Rules

- Every value is `unknown` until `records.ts` checks it ([ADR-0016](../../../../../docs/adr/0016-strict-typescript-and-type-aware-lint.md)). A write checks all its rows first; one out of shape refuses the whole write.
- A merge never lowers a number: X's counts only grow (ADR-0043, decision 6).
- The timeline's words are Xomoni's own or the person's note, at most 500 characters, never text from an X page (#146). They are shown as text, never as HTML.
- The vault's tables go with *Delete local data* and with `forgetAccount`. They are not in *Download a copy*.
- `db.ts`, `importer.ts` and `archive-parser.ts` use IndexedDB, so the content script must not import them; `types.ts`, `records.ts`, `archive-manifest.ts`, `csv.ts` and `analytics-csv.ts` are safe there (`timeline.ts` is not: it writes).
- A parser reads nothing before `planImport` has checked every size, and refuses another account's files before anything is written.
