# Code of Conduct

Xomoni follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior to **privacy@xomoni.com**. Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainer, who may remove content or block participants who break this code.
