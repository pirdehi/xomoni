# `shared/`: data model, parsers and storage

The kernel of the extension: the data model, parsers for X's GraphQL traffic and the X data archive, cleanup filters, pacing settings and the IndexedDB database. It is bundled into the dashboard page, the service worker and the content script in x.com tabs. It must never import another area, and it never acts on X. Files the content script imports (`attention.ts`, `graphql.ts`, `messages.ts`, `x-archive.ts`, `x-entities.ts`, `x-text.ts` and their imports) must not use IndexedDB or extension-only Chrome APIs, because there they run under x.com's origin.

| `vault/reach-compare.ts` | Reach's comparisons (#429, ADR-0045 point 2): `reachComparisons` compares the person's settled posts (not replies) with a link against those without, with photos or videos (no link) against text only, and with Premium against the 8 weeks before it (`PREMIUM_SIDE_DAYS`), leaving out the 7 days after it began (`PREMIUM_TRANSITION_DAYS`), from the latest start Xomoni knows; without one it asks for the date (`needsDate`). | `vault/reach-stats.ts` | The statistics of reach diagnosis (#429, [ADR-0045](../../../../docs/adr/0045-reach-diagnosis-guesses-labelled-as-guesses.md) point 2): `median`, `quantiles`, `formatOf` (reply, link, media, text, or unknown without the links and media of #428), `isSettled` (a number, read at least `YOUNG_POST_DAYS` (7) after the post), `baseline` (each post's rolling median of the settled posts of its format in the `BASELINE_DAYS` (56) before it, null with fewer than `MIN_GROUP` (15); one pass in time order), `vsBaseline`, and `compareGroups`: *not enough data* below 15 on a side, else each side's size and median, the ratio of the medians and its 95% interval from `RESAMPLES` (2,000) bootstrap resamples drawn with `seededRandom` (mulberry32, `DEFAULT_SEED`), so the same posts always give the same answer. `verdictOf` gives one of the five `REACH_VERDICT_WORDS` of `@xomoni/bridge`: the whole interval below 1 or above it is *clearly lower* or *higher*; inside `NO_DIFFERENCE_BAND` (0.8 to 1.25) *no difference*; otherwise *maybe*. | `vault/suspects.ts` | Suspected posts, as guesses (#429, [ADR-0045](../../../../docs/adr/0045-reach-diagnosis-guesses-labelled-as-guesses.md) point 4): `suspectPosts`, for each post label X applied to n posts in a month (UTC, as X's periods are), names that month's settled posts under half their baseline (`SUSPECT_BELOW`), lowest first, at most min(3n, 10) (`maxSuspects`), each with its numbers, its reasons in words and `guess: true`. ## Depends on

- No other area of the extension (rule `ext-shared-is-the-kernel`).
- npm: `dexie` (IndexedDB wrapper, in `db.ts`).
- Chrome APIs: `chrome.notifications` and `chrome.runtime.getURL` (in `notify.ts`).

## Rules

- `ext-shared-is-the-kernel` (from `.dependency-cruiser.cjs`): files here import only other files in `shared/` and npm packages.
- `ext-content-is-isolated` and `ext-inject-is-isolated`: page scripts may use `shared/` and nothing else, so anything they need from the rest of the extension has to live here.
- `no-circular`: imports inside `shared/` go one way, for example `filter.ts` → `db.ts` → `settings.ts` → `types.ts`.
- Safety items from [`docs/PLAYBOOK.md`](../../../../docs/PLAYBOOK.md) implemented here:
  - B4 (#30): `pacing.ts` sets the work sessions, rests and delay shape; `settings.ts` turns active hours on by default (8:00–23:00).
  - A1–A3 (ADR-0012): `x-limits.ts` holds X's published limits and derives every cap as one fifth of them (deletes: 10 per 15 min; unlikes: 200 a day, shared with ghost-like cleanup); `settings.ts` builds Careful from those ceilings.
  - D1, D3, D4: `attention.ts` detects a logged-out page, X's check pages and an account switch, so the engine waits for the person instead of acting.
  - E5: `upsertItems` in `db.ts` marks a deleted item `present` again only if X shows it more than 5 s after the deletion.
  - E8: `parseArchiveAccount` lets the importer refuse an archive that belongs to another account.
  - F4: `graphql.ts` finds operations by name and walks responses without depending on their exact shape.
  - Known gap D2: `isLoginPath` does not recognize X's `/i/jf/` login flow yet.
