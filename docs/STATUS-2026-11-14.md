### 12 to 14 November

- **The status report of 12 November** ([#194](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/pull/194)).
- **Live check of *Get my archive*** in the maintainer's Chrome ([#186](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/186#issuecomment-6865557463)): asking X, and recognizing the archive being made, work on the real page.
- **Downloads fixed** ([#196](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/issues/196), [#197](https://github.com/pirdehi/xomoni/pull/197)):
  - **The problem:** Chrome refuses Downloads itself for every extension (*"contains system files"*).
  - **The fix:** X's archive is now saved in **Downloads › Xomoni**. Xomoni makes that folder, and the person chooses it once.
  - **The new permission:** this takes Chrome's `downloads` permission. Xomoni uses it only to say where X's archive is saved; no other download is moved or recorded.
  - **Tested** in a real Chromium with its own Downloads folder.
- **Plain wording in the Activity log:** Chrome's messaging errors are now told in plain words.
- **The UX review of the dashboard, and Phase 12 built:**
  - *Settings:* *Careful* in five lines; the table of 35 fields only under *Custom*.
  - *Cleanup:* three choices, with *Exceptions* and *Advanced* folded.
  - *A banner when X needs you,* on every page.
  - *Home:* one status, its one button, and getting started.
  - *Activity:* Jobs and Log in one page; the menu has six places.
  - *Library:* one card, with the archive as the recommended way.
  - *The log:* runs folded into one line, with each post's text.
  - *Words and numbers:* *the X window* and *your copy* everywhere; each count says what it counts.
  - *A modern look* in light and dark.
