# `src/cloud`: job status for xomoni.com

Connects this browser to a xomoni.com account and keeps the website's dashboard up to date while the extension's dashboard is open ([ADR-0018](../../../../docs/adr/0018-pair-the-extension-with-a-one-time-code.md)). It sends **job status only**: Xomoni's own job titles, states, phases, counters, timestamps and each account's risk level. Never post text, likes, the inventory or X's notices ([ADR-0004](../../../../docs/adr/0004-x-data-stays-local.md)). It never acts on X, and nothing it receives can start work. In the same browser it also answers xomoni.com's reads of the Library, Watch and account health, straight to the page and never to a server ([ADR-0028](../../../../docs/adr/0028-xomoni-data-on-the-website-in-the-same-browser.md)).

| File            | Responsibility                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `config.ts`     | The public Supabase URL and publishable key (the same ones the website ships), and the page where codes are made.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `client.ts`     | `pairDevice`, `reportStatus`, `unpairDevice`: the three functions a device may call, as `fetch` to PostgREST with no session; `CloudError` with a reason (`bad_code`, `device_limit`, `unknown_device`, `unreachable`, `refused`). `reportStatus` also answers whether the account shows its daily numbers; `reportStats` and `setStats` call `device_report_stats` and `device_set_daily_stats` (#250), and `StatsRow` is a day as it travels.                                                                                                                                      |
| `summary.ts`    | `jobReport`: one job as the website stores it, with the reason always empty. Pure. Watch jobs (#158) are never reported: they only read and never end, and the `jobs` table doesn't allow their kind. Nor are archive jobs (#186), which only handle X's archive (ADR-0004). The phase goes in words (`phaseWords`: *Searching X*, *Visiting each known post*, *Page 3 of 12*, #205). |
| `device-name.ts` | `suggestedDeviceName`: "Chrome on macOS" from the user agent, the name a browser gets when none is typed. |
| `web-connect.ts` | One-click connecting (#228, [ADR-0024](../../../../docs/adr/0024-connect-a-browser-with-one-click.md)): `startWebConnect` stores a 128-bit nonce and returns `https://xomoni.com/connect#ext=<id>&nonce=<nonce>`; `pendingWebConnect` and `cancelWebConnect`; `parseWebPairing` and `fromWebsite`, which accept exactly `{type: 'xomoni-pair', code, nonce}` from xomoni.com's top frame; `connectFromWebsite`, which uses the nonce up (15 minutes at most), leaves a connected browser alone, pairs, confirms (the website named the account before the click) and answers `{ok, account}` or a reason. |
| `web-session.ts` | One sign-in with xomoni.com (#232, [ADR-0025](../../../../docs/adr/0025-one-sign-in-for-the-website-and-the-extension.md)): `extensionState` (version, connection without the token, signed-out time, `leftAt`), `signInFromWebsite` (pairs with a code from a signed-in page, replaces only the connection the page names, confirms at once, ends a sign-out of Xomoni), `signOutFromWebsite` (signs out of Xomoni with the data kept and ends the token; a browser without a connection is left alone), `leaveWebsite` (the person signs out of xomoni.com here: remembers when, then disconnects) and `websiteLink` (a xomoni.com page with this extension's id in the fragment). |
| `website.ts` | `answerWebsite`, the service worker's one entry for xomoni.com: only its top frame is answered, only the nine messages of `@xomoni/bridge`, and those that change the connection or act on a job run one after another; a settings change and a cleanup plan wait their turn too; reads change nothing and are answered at once. |
| `website-access.ts` | `accessRefusal`: why xomoni.com's action, read or settings change can't be taken here (`not_connected`, `signed_out`, `other_account`), the one check all use; `activeAccount`, the X account they are about. |
| `website-reads.ts` | xomoni.com's reads in this browser (#233, [ADR-0028](../../../../docs/adr/0028-xomoni-data-on-the-website-in-the-same-browser.md)), under the same checks as the actions. `readLibrary`: the active account's items of one kind, newest first, filtered by text or author, 100 at a time, with each kind's counts; `readHealth`: each account's risk level, pace and when a hold ends; `readWatch`: each search, when it looked and what it found; `readReplies` (#256): the active account's replies still on X, newest first, filtered by date, likes, the handle they answer, words and threads, 100 at a time, with the 20 people replied to most, in one pass over the replies. Texts are cut to 1,000 characters, and nothing is changed. |
| `website-plans.ts` | Cleanups planned on xomoni.com (#235): `toFilter`, `describePlan`; `estimatePlan`, how many items a plan matches in the active account's copy (`findCandidates`), whether its archive was imported, and the pace per day; `answerPlan` keeps the plan for the Cleanup page (`savePlannedCleanup`), logs it and opens that page. A plan of posts picked on the website keeps their ids (`onlyIds`, #256) and is logged as "N replies picked on xomoni.com". No job is made. |
| `website-settings.ts` | xomoni.com's settings in this browser (#234, [ADR-0029](../../../../docs/adr/0029-xomoni-settings-from-the-website.md)): `readSettings` (pace caps, active hours, notifications, keep going while away, failures in a row, the active account's Watch searches); `answerSettings` applies a change through `normalizeSettings` and `setWatchQueries`, writes *Settings changed on xomoni.com* to the log (`describeChange`), and answers the settings as stored. |
| `website-actions.ts` | xomoni.com's actions in this browser (#251, [ADR-0027](../../../../docs/adr/0027-extension-actions-from-the-website.md)), taken only when signed in to Xomoni and connected as the device the page named (`other_account` otherwise). `answerJob`: pause hands a running job's stop to the dashboard tab that runs it (`requestStop`); cancel does the same, or cancels a job that isn't running right here and reports it; resume never runs anything, it asks the person in Xomoni (`requestResume`) and opens the job list. `answerOpen` opens a page of Xomoni through the service worker's `ActionPorts`. Every action taken is written to the job's log. |
| `connection.ts` | The device id and token in the extension's IndexedDB (`kv`, key `cloud`), where content scripts can't reach them (#151); an older copy in `chrome.storage.local` is moved on first read. `watchConnection` for the UI.                                                                                                                                                  |
| `release.ts`    | The newest release, from `device_report`'s answer (#151): `parseReleaseNews`, `isNewer` (numbers, so 0.10.0 follows 0.9.0), and the notice the dashboard shows, which can be dismissed unless the release fixes a vulnerability.                                                                                                                                        |
| `daily-report.ts` | Daily numbers on xomoni.com, by choice (#250, [ADR-0026](../../../../docs/adr/0026-daily-numbers-in-the-cloud.md)): `statsShown`, `rememberStatsChoice`, `chooseStats` (on or off from the extension; off deletes on xomoni.com); `toStatsRow`, a day with exactly the ADR's columns; `sendFrom` and `sendDailyNumbers`, which send each X account's days not yet sent, 60 a call, back to 400 days the first time and from the day before the last one sent after that, and stop when xomoni.com says the numbers are off. |
| `sync.ts`       | `connect` (trade a code; nothing is reported until `confirmConnection`, which then reports the newest 50 jobs), `disconnect`, `syncOnce` (heartbeat plus jobs updated since the last report, 50 at a time) and `startCloudSync` (a report a few seconds after a job changes, and at least every 5 minutes). The person confirms the account pairing named first (#149). Each report also keeps the account's choice of daily numbers and, while it is on, sends the days not yet sent (`daily-report.ts`); connecting or disconnecting forgets the choice and what was sent (#250). |

## Depends on

- `../shared/` only (rule `ext-cloud-is-isolated`): the database, the session, the risk ladder and the JSON readers.
- `@xomoni/bridge`: the messages xomoni.com may send and their parsers.
- `fetch`, `chrome.runtime.getManifest`, and `chrome.storage.local` only to move a connection an earlier version kept there.

## Used by

- `../background/sw.ts`: `website.ts` only, for what xomoni.com asks, lending it `ActionPorts` to open Xomoni's pages (rule `ext-background-is-thin`, ADR-0024, ADR-0025, ADR-0027).
- `../ui/`: the Settings card that connects (on xomoni.com, or with a typed code) and disconnects, the account menu and the sign-out (#226), which disconnect too, and the app shell, which starts the sync. The engine never imports it, so a run on X never waits on the network to xomoni.com.

## Tests

- `../../tests/cloud.test.ts`: the client's requests and error reasons, the job report (no reason, valid handles only), the stored connection (in IndexedDB, moved from `chrome.storage.local`), connecting, reporting changes once, heartbeats, disconnection by the website, and a disconnect the website couldn't be told about (`FORGOTTEN_HERE`, #151).
- `../../tests/ui/cloud-card.test.tsx`: signing in and out of xomoni.com from Settings, and the refused-code message.
- `../../tests/web-session.test.ts`, `../../tests/website.test.ts`: the state a hello gets, signing in and out from the website, switching accounts, refusals, the sender checks and one message at a time (#232).
